my story

I started from scratch.
Twice.

When I came to Canada nobody here knew me. No Canadian experience, no network, just a degree from back home and a lot of stubbornness. So I started small and I kept going.

day one

New country, small jobs

I took the jobs I could get and learned how things work here. It was not glamorous, but it taught me to show up and figure things out fast.

2021

Pay2Day, Oshawa

My first finance job in Canada. I checked loan applications for risk and watched out for fraud and money laundering.

2022

Laurentian Bank, Toronto

Bigger bank, bigger data. Thousands of transactions a day, and my job was to find the ones that did not match and figure out why.

2025

Propel Holdings, Toronto

500 to 700 loan accounts every month. I sorted them by who was likely to default so we called the right people first. All by hand, in Excel.

then something changed

I used to make these decisions by hand. Now I teach machines to.

human judgment + machine scale

2026

Starting again, in AI

Somewhere in those spreadsheets I kept asking, why can a machine not learn this? So I went back to school. Now I am doing my Master of Business Analytics and AI at Ontario Tech, and I build the models that can do the work I used to do by hand.

next

My next role

I am looking for roles in AI, machine learning and data science, and I would love data analytics and business intelligence work too. I want to help teams use AI to automate slow, manual work, and build it so people can rely on it. Lately I am most excited about automating supply chain analytics, where forecasting and machine learning can take the guesswork out of ordering and inventory.

People ask me why I went back to school after all that. The answer is simple. I love a challenge, and this is what I really want to do. Starting over is scary, but I did it once and it worked. This time I am doing it for my passion, and that makes me really happy.
