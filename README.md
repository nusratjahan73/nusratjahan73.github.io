## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

I started from scratch. Twice.

When I came to Canada nobody here knew me. I took small jobs, then moved into finance: checking loan applications for risk and fraud at **Pay2Day**, matching thousands of transactions a day at **Laurentian Bank**, and sorting 500 to 700 loan accounts a month by default risk at **Propel Holdings**. All of it by hand, mostly in Excel.

Somewhere in those spreadsheets I kept asking, why can a machine not learn this? So I went back to school. Now I am doing my Master of Business Analytics and AI at Ontario Tech, and I build the models that can do the work I used to do by hand. I care just as much about whether people can trust them.

## 🌐 About This Website

My portfolio is built by hand with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so it runs on GitHub Pages with nothing to install and nothing extra to download.

**Try my model:** at the top of the page you can type a hotel review and a small Naive Bayes text classifier decides which team should handle it: Housekeeping, Front Desk, Restaurant or Maintenance. It shows how sure it is and underlines the words that made it decide, because a prediction should always come with a reason. It is trained on 80 example reviews and runs right in your browser. It is based on my Hotel Review Routing project.

**Other small touches:**
- Falling snowflakes in the background turn into a little network around your cursor
- A hand drawn timeline of my story that grows as you scroll
- My handwriting slowly turns into code: "I used to make these decisions by hand. Now I teach machines to."
- Project filters, and text that resizes so every line fits on any screen

```
index.html       the page and all the content
css/style.css    the design: colours, fonts, layout, phone sizes
js/main.js       the review model, the snowflake network and the animations
```
